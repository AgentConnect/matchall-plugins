Privacy Policy — review draft

1. Operator and contact

Matchall is operated by Zhang Yifan in the United Kingdom under the AWiki brand. For support and privacy requests, contact support@awiki.ai. The operator location does not mean data is stored only in the United Kingdom. Applicable detailed disclosures are being completed.

2. Information used

Account and authorization information includes an internal account identifier, OAuth grants and sessions, sign-in method, and an optional verified email. If Google sign-in is used, identity information is received as part of that sign-in. Matching information includes the text and structured fields you submit, lifecycle state, matching results, and participation or introduction records. Private contact instructions, their versions, and consent or revocation records are stored separately from business text. Support messages and limited operational/security records may also be retained.

Do not submit passwords, access tokens, payment-card information, government identifiers, health records, or personal information you have no right to share. Credentials should only be entered in the service's authentication interface.

3. Purposes and recipients

We use this information to authenticate and authorize access, prepare and match intentions, show relevant business summaries, process participation and mutually approved introductions, provide optional notifications, answer support requests, and protect the service.

Recipients include hosting/database and operational service providers, configured AI providers processing business text for extraction, embeddings and match assessment, mail providers delivering sign-in or notification messages, and the connected host such as ChatGPT receiving authorized tool results. Current production configuration identifies DeepSeek chat and Aliyun Bailian embeddings. The final processor list, contractual retention and processing locations remain to be verified; a model configuration labelled “local” does not mean the provider processes data locally.

Other matching participants may see necessary business summaries before contact approval. An intent is private by default; a separate approval of its current public preview makes the approved business summary available to the public-feed audience. Private contact fields are disclosed only through an authorized mutual exchange. Do not put contact details in business text, which may be processed as matching information.

5. Controls and requests

You can review a draft before activation, keep it private, separately approve public visibility, pause matching, and manage contact-sharing consent. Pausing, closing or fulfilling an intent is not account deletion. Revocation restricts future retrieval but cannot recall information already copied by another party. Disconnecting ChatGPT revokes that connection as supported by the authentication service; it does not by itself erase Matchall records.

Send access, correction, deletion or account-recovery requests to support@awiki.ai. We need a proportionate way to confirm account ownership; do not send login secrets. A request is not completed until its outcome is confirmed. Anonymous accounts may be unrecoverable if the browser session is lost and no recoverable identity was bound. The proposed procedure aims to respond promptly and normally within one calendar month; applicable verification, extensions and exceptions must be explained. It separates live-system erasure from restricted backups awaiting scheduled expiry. See the ICO erasure guidance. This procedure still needs implementation.

6. UK processing and rights — final factual review pending

The following purpose-to-basis mapping is a drafting proposal, not a completed legal assessment: providing the matching, account and introduction functions requested by a user may rely on performance of that user's service contract; proportionate security and abuse prevention may rely on legitimate interests after assessing necessity and the impact on individuals; separate optional disclosure choices need an appropriate documented basis and a way to withdraw where consent is relied upon. Do not treat accepting all terms as blanket consent for unrelated processing. Any legal-obligation basis must identify the actual obligation.

Depending on the applicable circumstances, individuals can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing; they can withdraw consent where that is the basis. Requests can be sent to support@awiki.ai. Rights are subject to their applicable conditions, and the response must explain any justified refusal or limitation. Individuals may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office; contacting Matchall first is not a condition of that right.

Matching uses automated extraction, representations and scoring to suggest counterparts. A score is not a transaction probability, a verification of a person's identity, or an approval of a purchase. Users review drafts, choose whether to activate matching and control introductions. The final notice must describe the actual role and effects of automation without claiming that all uses are exempt from applicable automated-decision rules.

Before publication, complete the service-provider and transfer record: actual hosting and processing countries, recipient roles, provider account settings, applicable transfer mechanism and how a person can obtain relevant safeguards. The operator's UK address and a model configuration labelled “local” do not establish where processing occurs. Do not claim UK-only storage, UK adequacy or signed transfer contracts without evidence. See ICO privacy-notice guidance.

7. Eligibility, changes and effective date

The operator has confirmed that the initial service is for users aged 18 or older. Age-access controls and appropriate age-assurance measures still require implementation and verification; this draft does not claim they are already active. Relevant processing locations and the detailed rights information remain to be completed. Material policy changes should be communicated through a suitable product notice and this page's updated date. This draft has no effective date.
