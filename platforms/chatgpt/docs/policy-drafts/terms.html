Terms of Service — review draft

1. Operator and eligibility

Matchall is operated by Zhang Yifan in the United Kingdom under the AWiki brand. Contact support@awiki.ai. The United Kingdom operator location is confirmed. Users must be at least 18 years old and able to agree to these terms. The operator approved this initial eligibility rule on October 21, 2026. This document remains a draft: corresponding age-access controls have not yet been implemented or verified. Detailed regional terms remain open; no particular UK court or legal jurisdiction is inferred from the operator location.

2. Service and price

Matchall provides free matching and introductions between needs and offers. It does not place orders or process payments. Participants arrange any transaction independently outside Matchall. A matching score, an introduction or a contact exchange does not confirm a sale or guarantee a person's identity, product quality, availability, legality or performance. Users should independently check material information before acting.

Matching uses automated extraction and assessment, which can be incomplete or wrong. Review drafts and unknown fields before activating an intent, and separately review any public preview or private contact disclosure.

3. User responsibilities

Submit information you have the right to use and share, keep relevant details accurate, and use the service lawfully. Do not impersonate others, harass participants, publish private information without permission, probe another account's data, bypass authorization or contact-consent requirements, distribute malware, or use the service for prohibited transactions or content. Do not include passwords or unrelated sensitive data in matching text.

We need permission to store and process submitted information to provide the requested matching and introduction functions, as described by the published privacy policy. This draft does not transfer ownership of your content or grant an unrelated use of it.

4. Consent and account control

Matching activation, public visibility and private contact exchange are separate choices. The tool or interface response determines the current state. Pausing stops matching while keeping the record. Closing and fulfilling an intent are distinct from deletion. Revoking access does not remove copies a recipient already obtained.

Use a recoverable sign-in method if ongoing access matters. An anonymous browser session is not a guaranteed account recovery mechanism. Account-support and data requests go to support@awiki.ai, not public issue trackers.

5. Availability and abuse handling

Proposed for operator approval: the operator may restrict activity that violates these terms or threatens service security, with an explanation and a support route where feasible and lawful. Changes to fees, material functionality or terms should be communicated before they apply. The service's current free model does not authorize charging users under this draft.

Specific suspension, complaint and account-closure procedures still need confirmation. No unilateral indemnity, liability cap, arbitration requirement or governing jurisdiction has been invented. Any final wording must preserve rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

6. Privacy and changes

The final terms will link to the published Matchall privacy policy. The retention targets were approved on October 21, 2026, as implementation goals. Their disclosure must distinguish actual capabilities from planned cleanup; approval does not mean that account erasure or backup expiry has been deployed. Closing an intention is not a deletion request or proof of erasure. Effective date, change-notice process, regional requirements and dispute handling are pending operator approval; this draft is not effective.
