Privacy Policy

Matchall is operated by Zhang Yifan in the United Kingdom under the AWiki brand. Zhang Yifan is responsible for the personal information processed to provide Matchall. Contact support@awiki.ai about this policy or your data.

SupportTerms of Service

1. Scope and information we use

This policy covers the Matchall website and its connections to hosts such as ChatGPT through MCP. Those hosts and independent services have their own privacy policies. Matchall is intended for people aged 18 and over.

  • Account and access information: account identifiers, sign-in method, optional email, authorization grants, sessions and, when you choose Google sign-in, the identity information needed to link that login.
  • Matching information: text you submit, structured fields and versions, lifecycle status, matching results, and interest, participation or introduction records.
  • Private contact information: contact instructions supplied separately from business text, their versions, and approval or revocation records.
  • Support and operation: messages you send us, request-handling records, and operational and security records needed to run and protect the service.

Information comes from you, the sign-in provider you choose, your authorized host, other participants' relevant business submissions, and service activity. We use cookies for sign-in and authorization, and browser storage for preferences such as language. Without the information needed for a requested function, we may be unable to provide that function. Binding an email is optional.

2. Purposes and legal bases

We process account and matching information to provide the service you request: authenticate access, prepare and match intentions, return results, manage participation and introductions, and provide associated notifications. The basis for processing necessary for these functions is performance of our service agreement with you.

Public visibility and private contact disclosure require your separate choices. We rely on consent for those optional disclosures; you can withdraw permission for future disclosure through the available controls or support. We rely on legitimate interests in protecting users and operating a reliable service for necessary security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting, and on applicable legal obligations when handling statutory data requests or required disclosures.

Matching uses automated extraction and scoring. Results are suggestions, not verified claims about another person, a decision to buy, or a guarantee of a transaction. You review drafts and decide whether to activate matching or approve an introduction.

3. Who receives information

Hosting, database, operational and email service providers process information needed for their functions. Configured AI services include DeepSeek for text processing, Alibaba Cloud Bailian for embeddings, and TypeSafe/Jev for matching decisions, with configured fallbacks. Business text and derived matching information may be sent to these services. Your connected host, such as ChatGPT, receives the results of authorized tool calls.

Matching participants can receive relevant business summaries before contact approval. Intentions are private by default: this keeps them off the public feed, but does not prevent the summaries needed for matching. Public visibility requires separate approval of the current preview. Private contact fields are disclosed through a mutually approved contact exchange.

Do not place private contact details in business text. Do not submit passwords, tokens, payment-card details, government identifiers, health records, or personal information you have no right to share. Information already copied by another participant cannot be recalled by revoking access.

4. Processing locations and providers

The operator's UK location does not mean that processing stays in the UK. The configured Alibaba Cloud embeddings endpoint is in its Beijing region; other infrastructure, AI and email providers may also process information outside the UK.

Our verification of every provider's processing location, retention settings and international-transfer arrangements is not complete. We do not claim UK-only storage, that a particular transfer safeguard covers every provider, or that all provider-held copies follow our local retention schedule. Contact support for the information currently available about a provider or a transfer. We will update this notice as these arrangements are verified.

Existing records and backups may remain beyond these target periods. Send a data request to support; this notice does not confirm that any particular record has been deleted.

We keep information for the purposes described above and review requests for earlier deletion individually. These targets are our operational choices, not uniform statutory UK retention periods.

6. Your choices and data requests

You can review a draft, keep an intention off the public feed, pause matching, and manage contact consent. Closing, fulfilling or pausing an intention is not account deletion. Disconnecting a host does not itself erase Matchall records. An anonymous account may be unrecoverable after its session is lost if no recoverable identity was linked.

Depending on the applicable circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. Email support@awiki.ai with your request and sign-in method. We may need proportionate information to confirm account ownership; do not send login secrets.

We will respond without undue delay, normally within one calendar month, explaining any applicable identity checks, extension, exception or limitation. Sending a request does not mean deletion is complete. We will explain the outcome, including any remaining backup or provider limitations. Our convenient email route does not exclude requests made through other legally valid channels.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. You do not have to contact us first to exercise that right.

7. Updates and contact

We will publish changes on this page with an updated date and provide an appropriate notice of material changes. Contact support@awiki.ai about privacy, account information or a suspected misuse of your data.

  • 账号与访问：账号标识、登录方式、可选邮箱、授权及会话；选择 Google 登录时，还包括关联登录所需的身份信息。
  • 匹配信息：你提交的文本、结构化字段与历史版本、意向状态、匹配结果，以及关注、报名、参与和引荐记录。
  • 私密联系信息：与业务文本分开提供的联系方式、版本及同意或撤销记录。
  • 支持与运行：你发来的消息、请求处理记录，以及运行和保护服务所需的运行与安全记录。

信息来自你选择的登录服务、授权客户端、你的提交、其他参与者的相关业务信息及服务使用过程。网站使用 Cookie 维持登录和授权，使用浏览器存储保存语言等偏好。缺少某项功能所必需的信息时，我们可能无法提供该功能。绑定邮箱为可选项。
