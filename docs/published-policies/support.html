MATCHALL BY AWIKI

How can we help?

For help with Matchall and its ChatGPT integration, email support@awiki.ai.

Contact support

Use this address for sign-in or account recovery questions, matching problems, contact-sharing concerns, privacy and data requests, and abuse reports. You do not need a separate ticket-system account.

Include the relevant feature, what you expected, what happened, and the approximate time with your time zone. If you include a screenshot, remove unrelated personal information.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, API keys, access tokens, payment card details, or another person's private contact information.

Account and privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, or account recovery requests, describe what you need and the account sign-in method. If your account has an email address, contact us from that address where possible. Do not send credentials. Sending a request does not itself delete data or establish that account recovery is complete.

Matching and contact sharing

Intents are private by default. Making an intent public requires a separate confirmation. Contact details are shared through a mutually approved exchange.

Pausing an intent stops matching while retaining the record. Closing or completing an intent is not account deletion. Revoking contact access restricts later retrieval; it cannot recall information someone has already copied.

Purchases and payments

Matchall provides free matching and introductions. It does not place orders or process payments. Participants arrange any transaction outside Matchall; matching and contact exchange do not confirm a purchase or guarantee a counterparty's claims.

ChatGPT connection issues

If authorization has expired, reconnect through ChatGPT's connection settings. If a tool fails, include the visible error message in your support request, after removing any secrets. Do not paste credentials into the chat.

MATCHALL 支持

需要帮助？

Matchall 网站及 ChatGPT 插件的问题，请发邮件至 support@awiki.ai。无需注册单独的工单系统。

问题反馈

此邮箱受理登录与账号恢复、匹配故障、联系方式交换、隐私与数据请求及滥用举报。请说明使用的功能、预期结果、实际情况及大致发生时间和时区。截图请先遮盖无关个人信息。

请勿发送密码、验证码、API Key、访问令牌、银行卡信息或他人的私密联系方式。

账号与数据请求

如需访问、更正、删除数据或恢复账号，请说明具体请求及登录方式；已绑定邮箱的账号，请尽量使用该邮箱联系。无需提供登录凭据。发送请求本身不代表数据已删除或账号已恢复。

匹配与联系授权

意向默认私密，公开展示需要另行确认；联系方式通过双方同意的交换流程提供。暂停匹配会保留记录，关闭或完成意向不等于删除账号。撤销联系授权限制后续读取，无法收回对方已复制的信息。

交易与付款

Matchall 提供免费撮合与引荐，不代下单、不处理付款。交易由参与者在平台外自行安排；匹配或联系方式交换不代表已成交，也不保证对方陈述属实。

ChatGPT 连接问题

授权过期时，请通过 ChatGPT 的连接设置重新授权。工具失败时，可在支持邮件中附上已移除敏感信息的错误提示。不要在聊天中粘贴登录凭据。
